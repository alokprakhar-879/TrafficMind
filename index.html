Formula reference

Algorithm & safety model

w ← w + α [r + 0.9 max Q(s′,a′) − Q(s,a)] φ(s,a)

Q(s,stage) = w · φ(s,stage): a linear function of 11 raw traffic features (queue, clearance time, oldest wait, current green, other queues, ambulance). Every 10 s the agent picks one of four protected stages; the same stage again extends green. Exploration decays from 1 to 0.05 and step size from 0.0005 to 0.00005 over the training budget. No hand-written rules, forecast, override or masks: everything is learned from reward. Yellow (3s) and all-red (2s) separate different stages.

Educational queue model, not a certified traffic controller. Assumes dedicated lanes and compatible paired protected turns; no pedestrians or downstream spillback.
